# Outcome: research scope after the prior art

Judged blind against `2026-11-23-scope-criteria.md`. Ranking: proposal 1 first, proposal 3 a close second, proposal 2 third. Proposal 1 won on falsifiability; 1 and 3 tied on novelty. Adopted: proposal 1, with the order of work and comparator choices the judge preferred from proposal 3, the settling launch from proposal 2, and proposal 2's perfect-information bound as a cost gate.

## Adopted decision

1. **The entropy claim is narrowed to two tests.**
   - B1, a check of a known decomposition, claimed as nothing new: with exactly interchangeable drugs and tied networks, the efficacy gap between drug-entropy and class-entropy SAC at temperatures fixed in advance equals the gap between the two soft-optimal schedules on the mean-field model (`experiments/predict_entropy_gap.py`), within an equivalence margin.
   - B2, the only part with new content: on a measured, partially overlapping cross-resistance matrix, SAC with the similarity-sensitive entropy H_Z lowers expected failure rate against drug-entropy SAC tuned at parity, or is equivalent to it within a margin; two-sided plus two one-sided tests. B2 waits for a prior-art search on similarity-kernel policy entropy. "At equal treatment cost" is dropped; no file defines a treatment cost.
2. **Claim A carries the paper:** a control characterisation of occupancy-coupled MDPs. At fixed (delay, memory, noise) the value of acting on the observed state splits into (i) the cost of certainty equivalence in the committed schedule (the noise-free plan against the best open-loop schedule under noise, by sample-average approximation), (ii) the true value of feedback (best open-loop schedule against the optimal closed-loop policy), and (iii) the suboptimality of certainty-equivalent replanning. On top sits the blind-derived boundary of `planned_once_minus_greedy`, tested at held-out (kappa, delta, horizon) against the theory-free null. Order: rung 1 in parallel with planner-only work on Claim A (read as not new method work); the noise-optimal reference; the Claim A preregistration; rung 2; the rung-3 bridge before any entropy test on test seeds; B1; parity tuning then B2; the completeness batch. The synthetic two-factor class-size sweep is dropped as a spine; under exact classes its outcome follows from the identity.
3. **Comparators.** Entropy tests: drug-entropy discrete SAC (SD-SAC stabilisers; temperatures per the temperature arena), MinRed SAC in both forms (known classes; learned inverse model), mechanism-level and two-level SAC, the alpha = 0 control (mandatory; the optimum is near-deterministic), the class-size-corrected variant, No Prior Mask only if code exists. Completeness: PPO with an entropy bonus; PPO-Lagrangian only if a preregistered constraint can bind; DQN (needed for rungs 1-3); SW-UCB, D-UCB and EXP3.S labelled stateless; delay-aware RL; memory-bandit planners. Non-learning references: uniform mixing over drugs and over mechanisms, cycling, the tuned rotation, myopic greedy, informed switching, the certainty-equivalent replanner, the sample-average open-loop schedule and the dynamic-programming reference. Each learned comparator gets the same dev seeds, configuration count and evaluation episodes, logged in `baselines/registry.md`.
4. **No true C in the networks in the main experiments:** one-hot or learned drug embeddings and the raw drug-indexed observation with the pending stack asserted; C enters only where a method is defined by it (OC-SAC's map, H_Z's kernel, MinRed's known-class form). The C-given configuration stays as B1's arm. Settled first by one dev launch: both conditions, PairedDrugs and one unequal-class instance, 10 dev seeds, temperatures and within-class entropy logged; if neither agent learns without C, this flips.
5. **Noise-optimal reference for small cells:** backward induction on a discretised state (two classes, delay at most 2), Gauss-Hermite quadrature over the shock, grid refined to a tolerance fixed in advance; the best open-loop schedule under noise by sample-average approximation. Gate first, minutes of planning: the perfect-information relaxation bound (Brown, Smith and Sun 2010); if the certainty-equivalent replanner sits within the phase test's margin of it, no new reference is needed. Check that can fail: within its policy class the dynamic programme must not lose to the replanner beyond Monte Carlo error. Placement in `ocmdp/baselines/` (owner-gated directory).
6. **Instance family for B2:** Oz et al. 2014 (22 drugs, measured normalised MIC matrix, Table S2), grouped by the paper's own Table 1 target column, n = (7, 5, 3, 3, 3, 1); C[a, j] = mean normalised resistance to the drugs of group j among populations evolved on drug a, clipped to [0, 1] (collateral sensitivity floored at 0, a stated limitation); H_Z kernel the cosine similarity of rows of C. Lazar et al. 2014 as a descriptive replication if its class list verifies. Planner restart agreement at K = 22 checked before the planner is used as a denominator. The data are promoted from the inbox before use; the NC licence bears on release.
