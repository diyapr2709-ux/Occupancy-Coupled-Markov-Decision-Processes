## Review of the step-1 diff, before any step-1 data

The adversarial reviewer and the stats verifier read the diff. The verifier recomputed `_classify` and `_step` independently and found no arithmetic discrepancy. Every fix below changes input checks or defect detection, not the convergence rule. All were made before any step-1 launch.

- **Fixed:**
  - **Planner reference.** The planner-restart report must:
    - come from the step-1 commit and code;
    - compare at least 5 planner seeds;
    - plan a gain over uniform.
    One seed gave a spread of 0, which passed vacuously.
  - **The owner's design is pinned.** The checker requires:
    - c-given;
    - dev seeds 0–4;
    - default delay and lam;
    - one agent configuration and the same `--set` across the six reports;
    - both agents at the same length;
    - a clean tree.
    Without this, a later relaunch on other seeds would have been checked as a fresh step 1.
  - **Divergence:**
    - a diverged run records the decision it diverged at (`diverged_at_decision`);
    - (a) compares that decision too;
    - at a fixed temperature runs are prefixes, so a divergence must appear at the same decision in every length that reaches it, and anything else is a DEFECT.
  - **Unscaled g.** A report whose replanner does not fail less than uniform mixing is a DEFECT. Before, g = inf or nan ended as UNDECIDED.
  - **Seed sets.** The comparison is keyed by report and variant. OC-SAC's seeds were being overwritten.
  - **No-summary launches.** A launch whose summary cannot be computed (every run of a variant diverged) still writes its report, marks that noise level `not_summarised` and exits 1.
  - **The pending-occupancy check.** It now runs delay + 1 quarters with a different drug each quarter. It compares against the drugs given, not the environment's own pending record.
  - **Step 2:**
    - reports a diverged run as a failure;
    - requires one platform fingerprint and the full canary across its reports.
  - **Printout.** The checker prints the mean and SD of d beside each interval.
  - **Names and wording.**
    - Person names in `decisions/2026-11-24.md` were reworded.
    - VERDICT.md says the harness "as read, would not" complete, and labels the factor of about 99 as derived.
- **Recorded, not changed:**
  - **The precondition is a proxy.** It measures the spread of the planned-once prediction from the nominal start. g divides by the every-quarter replanner's realised failure on the evaluation episodes, at one planner seed. No report measures the replanner's own restart error. That error moves the g > 0.5 threshold; the equivalence call is less affected, because R is shared across lengths.
  - **A known weakness of the adopted rule.** A step passes on the learned count at L. k(L') may fall by a(n) = 1 to below the majority. One instance can therefore converge at 24 000 with 2 of 5 learned at 48 000. Any report that uses L* states this.
  - **The owner's decision overrides two stops.**
    - The rung-1 verdict "not reproduced" is followed by steps 1 and 2 and later rungs.
    - That overrides CLAUDE.md's "report the size of the discrepancy and stop" and the step-1 outcome's "if rung 1 fails, the ladder stops".
    - The override is the owner's decision of 2026-11-26. CLAUDE.md itself is not edited here; that is the owner's to change.
  - **The c-given bit-identity check is a scratch check.** It is not a committed test: its hashes depend on the platform's arithmetic. The existing T1 test still checks the two agents against each other at temperature 0.
  - **Survivor means.**
    - `share_of_replanner_gain` averages completed runs only, so it is a survivor mean.
    - `runs_learned` and `runs` count diverged runs.
  - **A test job whose fingerprint differs from its launch's stops after the launch record is written.** That uses up the preregistration's test seeds. The driver's own check before the record makes this a mismatch between workers of one launch only.
