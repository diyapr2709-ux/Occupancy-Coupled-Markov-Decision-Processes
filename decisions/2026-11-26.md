- **Rule changes from the validator's 26 Nov findings, made before any step-2 report or held-out data existed:**
  - **Floor check (F2).** In `experiments/check_dev_protocol.py` step 2, the entropy-floor check is reported, not failed. A correct agent whose optimum rotates over unequal classes never visits its computed floor.
  - **Clamp margin (F9).** A learned temperature's window mean must stay at least 0.1 nats inside both clamp edges. Without that margin, a temperature pinned at the clamp for most of a window would pass.
  - **Unpredicted lines (F11).** In `experiments/score_phase_predictions.py`, a measured line that the predictions leave out has infinite error and is listed under `not_predicted`, so leaving lines out cannot lower E.
  - **Test-tier seeds (F5).** In `experiments/train_agent.py`, a test launch:
    - names its seeds explicitly;
    - names at least 10 of them;
    - uses exactly the set in the preregistration's one `seeds:` line;
    - reuses no seed from any earlier test-tier training launch record, whatever its preregistration.
  - **Where test launches write (F4).** A test launch writes only into a folder directly under `runs/`, where every later launch looks for earlier test launch records.
  - Decided by: the decision of 24 Nov that test preregistrations train on disjoint, named seeds, and the validator's demonstrations that the old rules failed correct runs or passed wrong ones.
