## C. Reporting practice (abstracts opened; RELATED, one line each on what it changes)

- **Shen, Zhang, Wang, "How Much Effort Does an AI Code Reviewer Need?", arXiv 2611.33639, 2026-11-25.** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33639
  - Claim: "Claude Opus 5.5 (preregistered), Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 (exploratory)", and the primary effect "cost 20% less, short of the preregistered 30%." (reported by).
  - Tag: RELATED.
  - What it changes: label every non-preregistered arm and cell as exploratory inside the abstract, not just in the body. Report a missed preregistered threshold as a miss, in those words.
- **Shao, Rezaei, Kamali, "What Does Matching a Human Intervention Effect Establish?", arXiv 2611.27186, 2026-11-20.** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27186
  - Claim: a preregistered audit beside an exploratory reanalysis, with the post-hoc additions named: "zero-reference check added after unblinding; norm beliefs emphasized after inspection".
  - Tag: RELATED.
  - What it changes: any check on the Claim A boundary added after the hashed predictions are unsealed gets an explicit "added after unblinding" tag next to it. Report the trivial-pass reference (here, the theory-free null) beside every agreement decision.
- **Liu, Wu, Ran, Li, "DriveOddsBench", arXiv 2611.29275, 2026-11-22.** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29275
  - Claim: "all confirmatory analyses were frozen before any test clip was measured". Model outcomes are scored against the rank distribution expected under the real distribution (a uniform null).
  - Tag: RELATED.
  - What it changes: state the freeze moment in the abstract, as in "before any test seed was run". Give the null's expected value beside the observed score, as they do with the 11.8% figure. This matches the project's blind-prediction protocol and changes only the wording.
- **Hofman, Chatzimparmpas, Sharma, Watts, Hullman, "Pre-registration for Predictive Modeling", arXiv 2311.18807, 2023-11-30** (seen but not opened in the previous pass; abstract opened now). https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.18807
  - Claim: "we propose adapting pre-registration practices from explanatory modeling to predictive modeling", with a lightweight template. Its named risks are "data-dependent decision-making, and unintentional re-use of test data".
  - Tag: RELATED.
  - What it changes: little. The abstract does not address exploratory/confirmatory separation. It can be cited for the test-reuse risk the seed tiers guard against.
- Seen in the preregistration listing only, not opened, no tag: 2611.21541 (SciResearch-Eval; abstract opened, but it describes a "pre-registered fresh split" and no reporting guidance, so it was not filed as practice) and 2611.32168, 2611.26383, 2611.21538.
